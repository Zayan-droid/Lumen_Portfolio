The Platform

One platform.
Four powerful perspectives.

Digitising every critical workflow across students, teachers, parents, and school administration.

Student Panel

Centralised access to daily schedules, pending homework, and academic records. Includes an AI-powered study assistant that provides guided hints without giving away the answer.

Teacher Panel

Equips educators with tools to manage attendance, distribute assignments, and generate one-click AI narrative performance reports for any student in their class.

Parent Panel

Delivers real-time visibility into a child's academic life. View live attendance, upcoming test dates, and receive instant absence alerts via direct notifications.

Admin Panel

Complete oversight of the institution's operations. Configure academic years, build timetables, manage users, and view institution-wide attendance analytics.
