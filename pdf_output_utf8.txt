Lumen  is a comprehensive, AI -powered school management platform purpose -built for private educational 
institutions. The platform digitises and streamlines every critical workflow across four key stakeholders ù 
students, teachers, parents, and school administrat ion ù delivering a unified and intelligent experience on a 
single, cloud -hosted solution.  
 
Lumen addresses a significant and largely underserved market gap in Pakistan's private education sector, 
where the majority of institutions continue to rely on manual record -keeping, fragmented communication 
channels, and reactive rather than data -driven d ecision -making.  
 
This document provides a complete overview of Lumen 's platform capabilities, technical architecture, artificial 
intelligence features, and commercial model, intended for the review of prospective institutional clients, 
investors, and distribution partners.  
 
2. The Problem We Solve  
 
Private schools across Pakistan face consistent operational challenges that impede academic outcomes and 
administrative efficiency:  
 
ò Attendance records are maintained manually, resulting in data errors and delayed parental notifications.  
ò Academic performance data is siloed  across teachers, making it difficult to identify at -risk students in 
time.  
ò Parents receive limited, infrequent updates on their child's academic progress and conduct.  
ò Teachers spend a disproportionate amount of time on administrative tasks rather than instruction.  
ò School leadership lacks real -time, consolidated analytics to make informed curriculum or resource 
decisions.  
 
Lumen resolves each of these challenges through an integrated, role -based digital platform enhanced with 
artificial intelligence.

Lumen's AI layer is purpose -designed for educational contexts. Rather than generic AI integrations, Lumen 
employs AI to deliver specific, measurable outcomes for students, teachers, and administrators.  
 
4.1  AI Student Performance Analysis  
The system automatically analyses a student's complete academic profile ù including attendance, quiz 
scores, assignment results, and grade history ù and generates structured, human -readable insight reports.  
 
Component  Description  
Input Data  Attendance records, quiz scores, assignment marks, subject grades  
AI Output  Narrative performance summary, identification of weak subjects, attendance impact 
analysis, personalised recommendations  
Trigger  Teacher -initiated ("Generate Report" button) or scheduled automated generation  
Recipient  Teacher dashboard, parent portal, school administration reports  
 
Sample AI output:  
Sara demonstrates exceptional aptitude in Science and Mathematics, consistently achieving 
above -average scores. Her performance in Urdu requires targeted attention, particularly in 
written expression. Attendance has remained exemplary at 90% this term. It is recommended 
that Sara undertake structured Urdu writing practice and regular grammar review to bring her 
performance to an equivalent level across all subjects.  
 
4.2  AI Homework Tutor (Student -Facing Chatbot)  
The AI Homework Tutor operates as a guided learning companion ù not an answer engine. It is 
architecturally designed to support academic integrity while providing meaningful learning support.  
 
ò Delivers step -by-step concept explanations tailored to curriculum level  
ò Provides structured hints to guide independent problem -solving  
ò Generates practice questions on demand by topic and difficulty  
ò Blocks requests for complete assignment solutions to uphold academic integrity  
ò Response length and scope are intelligently limited to prevent misuse
